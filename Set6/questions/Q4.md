# Set 06 - Question 4: Product Popularity Ordering

**Difficulty:** Medium (26 marks)

## Scenario
An online store logs the product ID of every sale. A 'rarity report' lists the sales so that the least frequently sold products come first. Products sold equally often are listed with the larger product ID first. Each sale is listed individually, so a product sold three times appears three times in a row.

## Problem Statement
Given the n product IDs of the sales, output all n IDs arranged so that (1) IDs with smaller total frequency come first, and (2) among IDs with the same frequency, the larger ID comes first. All copies of one ID are consecutive. Built-in sorting, Counter and sorted() with a key are not allowed; write your own sorting routine with a custom comparison.

## Input Format
- Line 1: integer n.
- Line 2: n space-separated integers, the product IDs.

## Output Format
- Print the n IDs in the required order on one line, separated by spaces.

## Constraints
- 1 ≤ n ≤ 10^5
- 1 ≤ ID ≤ 10^9

## Sample Input
```
9
4 4 6 2 2 2 6 9 6
```

## Sample Output
```
9 4 4 6 6 6 2 2 2
```
